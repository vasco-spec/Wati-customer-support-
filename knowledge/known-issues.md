## Weekly rhythm

- **Monday is payment day, and Tuesday is busy too.** Fleets pay their drivers on Mondays, so the inbox is busiest on **Monday morning**: data not imported yet, values that don't match, reports, payment files. Check the inbox every 5 minutes on Monday and Tuesday mornings (8:00–13:00), every 10 minutes otherwise, and don't leave any conversation unattended (Vasco, 17 Nov 2026). **Wednesday to Sunday** are quieter: checking every **25 minutes** is enough (Vasco, 18 Nov 2026). **After 18:00** (every day), checking **hourly** is enough (Vasco, 18 Nov 2026).

## Recurring situations (not bugs)

| Situation | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Uber data not available yet on Monday morning** (e.g. "a leitura da Uber está com erro") | The weekly Uber import is still running | Tell them to wait and keep refreshing the page; it should be ready **before 10:00**. Draft: "Quando os dados da Uber ainda não estão disponíveis, basta aguardar e ir atualizando a página. Deverão ficar disponíveis antes das 10h." Escalate only if it's still missing after 10:00 (confirmed by Vasco, 16 Nov 2026) |
| Uber/Bolt/Prio data still not imported after 10:00 on Monday | Platform login expired, or needs a verification code | Escalate. The team requests the code and the customer forwards it |
| Sync stops after the customer **changes a password** | We log in with the old credentials | Escalate. Manual re-connection with the new password and an SMS code |
| Uber SMS code doesn't arrive | Uber SMS can be very slow | Reassure them: it usually arrives. If it expires, the team requests another |
| Via Verde differs from the week / big differences for some drivers | Via Verde billing lags; we only use the data available on Via Verde. Delayed movements (**retroativos**) appear later | Explain (see FAQ). We can't change it: the retroativos have to be passed to the drivers when they become available on Via Verde, otherwise the fleet loses that money. Draft: "Infelizmente não conseguimos fazer nada quanto a isso. O sistema usa os dados disponíveis na Via Verde, e quando há atrasos (retroativos) esses movimentos só aparecem mais tarde." + "Quando ficam disponíveis, têm de ser passados aos motoristas, senão é a frota que perde esse dinheiro." (confirmed by Vasco, 18 Nov 2026) |
| Uber payout to the bank ≠ total "Uber Líquido" in the calculation (small difference) | Compare each driver's Net earnings on the Uber page with Uber Líquido; usually one driver has a payment adjustment, e.g. a trip done at the weekly cut-off | Tell the customer which driver and the difference, and that Uber manages these adjustments itself: if it paid more this week, it pays less in another. If they ask whether to adjust the driver's payment: "pela nossa experiência não é necessário" (always "from our experience", so it isn't binding). Balance Uber keeps in the Uber Fleet account: beyond our knowledge, they should contact Uber (confirmed by Vasco, 16 Nov 2026) |
| Uber amount ≠ Uber Earnings page | Adjustments from previous weeks, refunds, or a different date range | Ask them to compare the exact same dates. Escalate if it still differs |
| Same driver on two lines | Different names on Uber and Bolt | Merge video (FAQ §3) |
| Report doesn't reflect a change | Reports have to be regenerated | Refresh, then generate again |
| Driver doesn't receive the report on WhatsApp | The driver must message our number first; sometimes WhatsApp needs reinstalling | FAQ §3, escalate if it persists |
| Can't make payments | Revolut/bank authorisation expired | Escalate |
| Prio Elétrico (or other fuel) total in the portal is higher than in the system for the week | A charge made **almost at the weekly cut-off** (e.g. Sunday ~22:45) wasn't available yet when the payment was done on Monday | Find the charge that matches the difference and tell the customer not to worry: it will be charged **next week**. Ask Raffa on the next Monday to confirm it came in (confirmed by Vasco, 17 Nov 2026) |
| Prio Elétrico card is assigned to the driver but its charges don't come in | The fleet hasn't given (or has withdrawn) the **consent to GalgoSheets in the Prio Elétrico portal**. Without it we can't fetch the charges | Tell the customer the consent is missing and ask if they want us to do it for them: "o valor não entrou porque ainda não foi dado o consentimento à GalgoSheets no portal da Prio Elétrico... Quer que tratemos disso por si?" (confirmed by Vasco, 24 Nov 2026) |
| Via Verde not importing after the customer **changed the Via Verde password** (before Monday) | Our system keeps trying to log in with the old password, so **Via Verde blocks the access** for a while | Explain it is temporarily blocked. Two options: **wait**, or **pay now without Via Verde**; next week the system charges this week's Via Verde together with the next week's (confirmed by Vasco, 24 Nov 2026) |
| Prio diesel date is old | It shows the last refuel date | Expected |
| "Dados não disponíveis" warning | No charges that week on that source | Check before escalating |
| Warning in Calcular Pagamentos: "Detetámos colunas no cálculo que podem não estar a ser consideradas no pagamento final" (e.g. **Dívida**) | A column (like Dívida) isn't included in the total formula, so the report shows it but doesn't deduct it | The team fixes the formula and generates the report again. Tell the customer it's fixed and the new report is ready. If the warning was there in an earlier week with nothing in that column, there was no impact (confirmed by Vasco, 16 Nov 2026) |
| Rent (aluguer) looks wrong, e.g. much lower than usual | Last week the rent was **prorated** because the driver only worked part of the week (e.g. 2 of 7 days of 350€ = 100€), and that value stayed | Before escalating, look at past conversations/weeks for a prorated or manual value. The normal weekly value applies again (confirmed by Vasco, 16 Nov 2026) |
| "Should have deducted X€ of debt, only deducted Y€" | The Mapa de Dívidas holds a different debt than the customer thinks (e.g. ID-7 owed only 15€ from last week) | Check the Mapa de Dívidas for that driver and week first. Explain what it shows, and send the debt-map and manual-debt videos so they can change it themselves. **Debts from past weeks can't be edited.** To increase it, add the extra amount as debt in **last week's** column (e.g. +15€ on 02/11), as in the video, then generate the report again. If the map is right but the report still doesn't deduct, check the **"Dívida (€)" column in Cálculo**: that's the amount charged this week. An "M" means it was set manually (e.g. 0,00€), so nothing is deducted and the debt carries over. Put the amount there and generate again (confirmed by Vasco, 16 Nov 2026) |
| "This driver has no debt but debt is showing" | The driver's **total this week is negative**; the negative amount moves to Dívida Acumulada to be deducted in the next weeks. It isn't in the Mapa de Dívidas yet because it's this week's | Explain it, and ask what the correct value should be if they think this week's total is wrong (confirmed by Vasco, 16 Nov 2026). **A payment registered in the Mapa de Dívidas only affects the following week**: this week still shows the debt, next week "Dívida Sem. Passada" will be 0€ |
| A scheduled charge (installment, IUC, loan…) isn't being charged this week | It was set to **start on the current date** (e.g. 16/11), but on Monday we're paying the **previous week** (e.g. week of 09/11) | Tell the customer the start date is next week's; if they want it charged this week, set the start date to the week being paid (e.g. 09/11) and generate the report again (confirmed by Vasco, 16 Nov 2026) |
| Fuel from a supplier we don't integrate with (e.g. **Alves Bandeira**) is missing | We don't know how that supplier's system works | Ask the customer, politely, if they wouldn't mind adding those values manually: it's easy (confirmed by Vasco, 16 Nov 2026) |
| Values change "by themselves", a manual entry nobody recognises (e.g. in the Mapa de Cauções), or the report sent is the old one even after regenerating it | **Several people or computers on the Pagamentos page at the same time.** Even if the other person isn't editing, just having the payments page open on another computer is enough to cause differences between what was edited and what the report shows | Ask **how many people use the system at the same time** (e.g. one person edits, another does the payments). Explain the cause and recommend only one computer with the payments page open at a time. Fix the value (or ask the team to), and ask them to send us the **concrete example** next time it happens, so it's easier to see what went on (confirmed by Vasco, 16 Nov 2026) |

**POP IVA = 0 with "IVA indicado pelas plataformas"** (bug, SCRUM-151): POP doesn't report the VAT it charged, so fleets on that IVA option get POP IVA = 0. Until fixed, Raffa corrects it manually: POP VAT computed by us on (bruto − gorjetas − portagens). Holding reply + tag Raffa in #cs (confirmed by Vasco, 23 Nov 2026). Week of 16/11: a manual **IVA_pop** column was added for affected fleets; the fix is expected from the following week. Tell the customer: "esta semana adicionámos manualmente uma coluna IVA_pop... Basta atualizar a página." (confirmed by Vasco, 23 Nov 2026)

**Re-sent reports keep the old values** ("altero um valor, envio novo relatório e os valores antigos não são substituídos"): happens when **several people use the account at the same time**. If anyone has the **Cálculo** page open, even without using it, it can affect report generation. **Answer:** "Isso acontece porque há várias pessoas a usar o sistema ao mesmo tempo. Se alguém tiver a página Cálculo aberta, mesmo sem a estar a usar, pode afetar a geração dos relatórios. Antes de alterar valores e gerar os relatórios, confirme que mais ninguém tem o Cálculo aberto." (confirmed by Vasco, 23 Nov 2026) **This is the only known cause.** If the customer says nobody else had it open, don't escalate to Raffa: ask for a concrete example (driver, week, value changed, value in the report) if they want help (confirmed by Vasco, 23 Nov 2026).

**Customer deleted a driver by mistake and wants it back** ("como posso recuperar um motorista que eliminei"): the customer can't undo it; **Raffa** restores it. Ask which driver (name), send a holding reply, and tag Raffa in #cs with the fleet and driver name. When Raffa confirms, tell the customer it's restored and "Basta atualizar a página." (confirmed by Vasco, 25 Nov 2026)

**Customer asks to "add" a platform's value** (e.g. "Podem adicionar o valor da Radius?"): check the fleet's total formula (`ver_formulas_total`). If the platform **is in the formula**, they mean **missing data**: holding reply and tag **Raffa** in #cs to run the data sync. If it **isn't**, it's a formula change: tag **Vasco** with the current formula. **GPS platforms (Radius Velocity, Cartrack, Kinesis Pro, Verizon) only bring kms and are never in the formula**, so "Radius" in a value/cost question means **Radius Combustível** (confirmed by Vasco, 25 Nov 2026).
